About EE Exam Pro – Online Electrical Engineering Mock Test Portal

EE Exam Pro is a free online practice platform built for Diploma and B.Tech Electrical Engineering students preparing for competitive technical exams like OPTCL Junior Engineer (JE), SSC JE Electrical, OSSC, and state power utility recruitments.

Covered Electrical Engineering Subjects & MCQ Topics

  • Power Systems: Generation, Transmission & Distribution, Fault Analysis, Load Flow, and Economic Dispatch.
  • Electrical Machines: Transformers, DC Machines, Three-Phase Induction Motors, Alternators, and Synchronous Motors.
  • Switchgear & Protection: Circuit Breakers (SF6, Vacuum, Air Blast), Protective Relays, and Substation Protection.
  • Control Systems & Power Electronics: Time & Frequency Response, Stability Analysis, SCR, Inverters, and Choppers.
